## Overview

At the LSU XR Studio I work across the virtual-production pipeline: real-time environments for the LED wall, interactive backgrounds, motion capture, lighting control, and production work on camera.

## LED-wall productions

### The Plaqueromancer

A puppet show for kids, performed in front of virtual backgrounds built in Unreal Engine and displayed on the LED wall.

### Phantom of Utopia III

A background animation in Unreal Engine featuring more than 80 photogrammetry-based poses.

<Video id="hdJBMVLngIA" caption="Phantom of Utopia III" />

### Interaction

An interactive background built in TouchDesigner that moves with live music.

### Hall of Mirrors

An interactive background that feeds the camera's image back into the screen, producing an endless recursion of frames.

<Video id="BWHbBDS_O40" caption="Hall of Mirrors" />

## Studio systems

### DMX lighting controller

I connected the studio's different light types to their controllers and built a **DMX controller inside Unreal Engine** for the SkyPanel lights.

### Motion capture

Installing and operating the studio's motion-capture system: OptiTrack cameras with Motive.

## On camera and behind it

I'm one of the videographers for LSU sport marketing and for studio interviews — lighting, camera and sound — and I played one of the lead roles in the short film *Lost Reveries*, as a doctor raised from the dead.
