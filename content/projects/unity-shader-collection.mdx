## Overview

A growing collection of shaders written by hand in ShaderLab and HLSL for Unity's built-in render pipeline. Each lives in its own folder with example materials, and a single demo scene previews them all. They're small on purpose — each isolates one idea worth understanding properly. Several of them grew out of production work, like the [nested stencil reveal](/work/ultrasound-procedure) in the ultrasound trainer.

| Shader | Idea |
|---|---|
| Displacement | Vertex displacement from radial cosine waves |
| Explosion wave | A distortion wave expanding from a centre |
| Stencil layers | Multi-layer stencil-buffer transitions |
| Plants | Alpha blending vs alpha clipping for foliage |
| Snow on top | Normal-based snow coverage with a controllable blend band |
| Blend, gradients, math VFX | Compositing modes, UV-driven gradients, math-driven patterns |

## Snow on top

Upward-facing surfaces turn white. The world-space normal's Y component says how much a surface faces the sky; an **inverse lerp** remaps that into a 0–1 blend between two artist-controlled thresholds, so the snow line can be soft or sharp.

The displacement shader moves vertices in the vertex stage: distance from the UV centre drives a travelling cosine wave, attenuated towards the edges.
