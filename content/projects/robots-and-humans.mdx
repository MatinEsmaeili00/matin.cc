## Overview

After several years of building digital twins, we're extending them to physical robots. The goal is a platform where people control robots through AI-powered voice or text commands, monitor each robot's state in real time, and see its actions mirrored in both the digital twin and the real world.

Simulation is central: robots can practise difficult tasks safely in the twin first, and once a behaviour is learned and validated virtually, the same behaviour is transferred to the physical robot.

The project is at an early stage; these prototypes show the interface and interaction ideas we're pursuing.

## Goals

- Assign tasks to robots with AI commands, by voice or text, alongside manual control.
- Monitor every robot's position, status, target and current action.
- Support robots from different manufacturers on one platform.
- Synchronise physical robots with their digital twins in real time.
- Test and train in simulation before real-world deployment.
- Reconstruct real environments with drones, photogrammetry and 3D scanning.

## From AI command to action

In the [Digital Twin Platform](/work/digital-twin-platform) we built a local AI assistant on an NVIDIA DGX Spark that understands voice commands, answers users and triggers actions inside the twin. The plan is to extend it so those commands drive physical robots while the application monitors their actions live — the first working slice of that is [Robot Voice Control](/work/rosorin-voice-control).

## Scanning the world in

Robots need accurate environments to rehearse in, so we capture real spaces with drones, photogrammetry and 3D scanning, then use them as navigation and task-practice environments.
