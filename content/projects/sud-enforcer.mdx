## Overview

Sud Enforcer was made by a team of three in 48 hours at the LSU Global Game Jam 2025, on the theme *Bubble*.

You are a soap bubble trying to cross the length of a kitchen counter to reach the sink full of dirty dishes. Avoid obstacles and sharp, pointy objects that will pop you — and don't take too long, because soap bubbles shrink and pop on their own over time.
