## Overview

Jewel Seeker is a Unity game made as a team project. The player makes their way through a terrifying land, collecting jewels to purify it while avoiding enemies. It's playable in the browser on Unity Play, and the code and its history are on [GitHub](https://github.com/MatinEsmaeili00/Jewel-Seeker).

I worked on it as one of the developers on the team.

## Technical breakdown

- **Enemy AI.** Enemies move with grid-based, A*-style pathfinding (`GridPathfinder2D`) and with Unity NavMesh agents. The Observer enemy runs a state machine with follow, agro, attack and flee states.
- **Player movement.** Stamina, dash and crouch.
- **Levels.** Three layered levels with gems to pick up along the way.
