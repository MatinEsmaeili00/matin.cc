## Overview

No Surprises! was built by a team of four in 48 hours at the Chillennium 2025 game jam. The theme was *In Plain Sight*, so we hid things that are only visible from exactly the right angle: the player switches the camera between 3D geometric planes used as axes, discovers surprise gifts that only appear from particular views, and pops them.

## Gameplay

Find and pop every gift. A map shows how many are left and which rooms they're likely in. Two pressures keep it tense:

- **Time** — two minutes to pop everything before Mom shows up and catches you red-handed.
- **Precision** — miss a gift and the noise may give away where you are.

## Camera system

The game hinges on the camera snapping cleanly between planes so that hidden geometry lines up into a recognisable gift.
